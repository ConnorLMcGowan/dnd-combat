4. TROUBLESHOOTING

⚠️ Model appears PINK

This usually indicates a material or shader compatibility issue.
Make sure your project is using URP and that the character materials use shaders compatible with your installed Unity/URP version.

⚠️ Animation doesn't work / Character is twisted

Check the FBX Rig settings and make sure the character is configured as Humanoid.
Also verify that the correct Avatar is assigned to the Animator.

⚠️ Materials or shaders look incorrect

Material and shader behavior can vary between Unity and URP versions.
If necessary, update or reassign the material using shaders compatible with your current Unity version.
Note: Troubleshooting steps may vary depending on the Unity version and render pipeline configuration.
